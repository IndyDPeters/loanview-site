Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what data the LoanView app collects, why, and what you can do with it. LoanView is made by Indy Peters. Contact: indy.peters@me.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never stored in plain text.
  • Loan data: loans you create or join, including amounts, descriptions, dates and who is lender, borrower or viewer.
  • Repayments: amounts, descriptions, payment references and, if you choose to add one, a screenshot of the payment.

Photos are only accessed when you choose to attach a screenshot. We do not collect your location, contacts or device identifiers.

Why we use it

Only to provide the app: to log you in, show your loans, and share a loan with the people you invite. We do not use your data for advertising, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your data.

Who can see it

People you invite to a loan can see that loan and its repayments. Viewers cannot see payment screenshots; only the lender and the borrower can. Your data is stored with our hosting and database provider (Supabase), who processes it on our behalf.

How long we keep it

We keep your data as long as you have an account.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app: tap your initial on the dashboard, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, the loans you lent out (with their repayments and screenshots) and your own repayments, and removes you as borrower from other people's loans. You can also email us to request deletion.

Children

LoanView is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
