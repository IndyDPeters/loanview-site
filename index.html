Frequently asked questions

How do I add someone to a loan?

Open the loan and tap Invite the borrower or Invite someone to look along. Share the code; the other person enters it under Join with code on their dashboard.

How do I record a repayment?

As the borrower, open the loan and tap Record a payment. You can add a payment reference and a screenshot of the bank transfer as proof.

Does LoanView move money?

No. LoanView only keeps track of loans and repayments. Payments themselves happen outside the app, for example by bank transfer.

How do I delete my account?

Tap your initial in the top right of the dashboard, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and the loans you lent out.
